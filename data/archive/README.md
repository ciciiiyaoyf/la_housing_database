# Superseded source vintages

Historical source material retained to explain earlier builds and methodological
changes. Superseded does not mean disposable: archived raw vintages remain immutable.

[deprecated_zori_nsa/](deprecated_zori_nsa/README.md) holds the retired non-seasonally
adjusted Zillow rent vintages. Their archive and deprecation records explain why
they are excluded from active ingestion. The active rent index is the smoothed,
seasonally adjusted all-homes-plus-multifamily ZORI product.
