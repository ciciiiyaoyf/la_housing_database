Assumption/proxy variables\n\nAggregate Mortgage Debt\n\nVariable:\n\nacs_implied_aggregate_mortgage_debt_proxy\n\nHow it is built:\n\n1. Use ACS B25081 for owner-occupied units with mortgage.\n\n2. Use ACS B25106 owner income × housing-cost-burden bins.\n\n3. Allocate B25081 mortgaged units across positive-income B25106 bins proportionally.\n\n4. Infer monthly owner housing cost: income midpoint × cost-burden midpoint / 12\n\n5. Treat 80% of owner housing cost as mortgage debt service.\n\n6. Convert monthly payment to implied balance using a fixed-rate amortization formula.\n\n7. Use Freddie Mac PMMS average 30-year mortgage rate over the ACS 5-year window.\n\n8. Assume 25 years remaining term.\n\n9. Sum all bin-level balances.\n\nWhy:\n\nACS does not publish mortgage balances. It publishes mortgage-status counts and owner cost-burden distributions, so this is the closest public county-level workaround.\n\nMain assumptions:\n\n* Income midpoints: $10k, $27.5k, $42.5k, $62.5k, $112.5k\n\n* Cost-burden midpoints: 10%, 25%, 40%\n\n* Debt-service share: 80%\n\n* Remaining term: 25 years\n\n* Zero/negative income bins are preserved but excluded from balance inference.\n\nDocumented in:\n\nmetadata/proxy_methodology_assumptions.csv\n\n\n\n\n\nAggregate Home Equity\n\nVariable:\n\nacs_implied_aggregate_home_equity_proxy\n\nFormula:\n\nACS owner-occupied housing value proxy - aggregate mortgage debt proxy\n\nwhere:\n\nowner-occupied housing value proxy = ACS owner-occupied units × ACS median home value\n\nWhy:\n\nThere is no public LA County household balance-sheet series for aggregate home equity. This follows the sheet’s workaround: housing value minus debt.\n\n\n\n\n\nMonthly Mortgage Payment\n\nVariables:\n\nmortgage_payment_3_5pct_down mortgage_payment_10pct_down mortgage_payment_20pct_down\n\nFormula:\n\nmonthly principal-and-interest payment = amortized loan payment on Redfin median sale price\n\nAssumptions:\n\n* Down payment scenarios: 3.5%, 10%, 20%\n\n* Loan term: 30 years\n\n* Rate: Freddie Mac PMMS monthly average 30-year fixed rate\n\n* Excludes taxes, insurance, HOA, maintenance, and PMI\n\nWhy:\n\nActual realized mortgage payments are not public at LA County monthly frequency. These are affordability scenarios, not observed payments.\n\n\n\n\n\nDownpayment\n\nVariables:\n\ndown_payment_3_5pct down_payment_10pct down_payment_20pct\n\nFormula:\n\nRedfin median sale price × down payment share\n\nWhy:\n\nDown payment amounts are not publicly observed by county/month. These scenarios represent common affordability benchmarks: FHA-style low down, intermediate, and conventional 20%.\n\nClosing Costs\n\nVariables:\n\nclosing_cost_2pct closing_cost_3pct closing_cost_5pct\n\nFormula:\n\nRedfin median sale price × closing cost share\n\nWhy:\n\nPublic geography-specific closing-cost data are not available. The percentages give low/central/high scenario estimates.\n\n\n\n\n\nSingle-Family Housing Starts proxy\n\nVariable:\n\npermits_1_unit\n\nHow used:\n\nCensus BPS 1-unit permits are used as the public proxy for single-family starts.\n\nWhy:\n\nThe available public source is permits, not starts or completions. The database explicitly labels this as permits and does not relabel permits as starts.\n\n\n\n\n\nNet Migration / Immigration workaround\n\nMain variable:\n\nirs_soi_net_migration_exemptions\n\nFormula:\n\nIRS SOI in-migration exemptions - IRS SOI out-migration exemptions\n\nAlso loaded:\n\nirs_soi_net_migration_returns irs_soi_net_migration_agi\n\nWhy:\n\nIRS SOI county migration is the official public annual county migration workaround. Exemptions approximate people better than returns. Returns approximate filing units/households. AGI captures income composition.\n\nLimitation:\n\nThis is tax-return migration, not complete immigration. It misses nonfilers and does not fully capture international migration.\n\n\n\n\n\nPrice-to-Rent Ratio\n\nVariable:\n\nprice_to_rent_ratio\n\nFormula:\n\nZHVI / (12 × ZORI)\n\nWhy:\n\nThis gives a monthly price-to-annual-rent affordability ratio. It uses the active Zillow ZORI smoothed seasonally adjusted series. This is implemented as an alternate source version; the FHFA/ZORI version from the sheet is still not separately constructed.\n\n\n\n\n\nRental inventory\n\n– redfin or zillow?\n
