CURRENT METHOD PRECEDENCE (2026-08-02)

The Version A variables ending in `_benchmark` are the authoritative mortgage-debt and home-equity standard. They use direct ACS B25089/B25081/B25082/B25120 levels, weighted ACS PUMS parameters, exact-date NMDB controls when compatible, and clearly labeled PMMS sensitivity fallbacks. The older B25106 midpoint / 80% debt-service / 25-year method documented below is retained only as a legacy modeled scenario for reproducibility.

The HUD-USPS `usps_residential_vacancy_rate` method is a quarterly all-residential administrative address-vacancy proxy, not true rental-listing inventory. Its surviving 2026-08-02 package is quarantined and excluded from active facts until official raw responses and crosswalk provenance are reacquired and archived. No-stat remains a separate diagnostic.

Assumption/proxy variables

Aggregate Mortgage Debt

Variable:

acs_implied_aggregate_mortgage_debt_proxy

How it is built:

1. Use ACS B25081 for owner-occupied units with mortgage.

2. Use ACS B25106 owner income × housing-cost-burden bins.

3. Allocate B25081 mortgaged units across positive-income B25106 bins proportionally.

4. Infer monthly owner housing cost: income midpoint × cost-burden midpoint / 12

5. Treat 80% of owner housing cost as mortgage debt service.

6. Convert monthly payment to implied balance using a fixed-rate amortization formula.

7. Use Freddie Mac PMMS average 30-year mortgage rate over the ACS 5-year window.

8. Assume 25 years remaining term.

9. Sum all bin-level balances.

Why:

ACS does not publish mortgage balances. It publishes mortgage-status counts and owner cost-burden distributions, so this is the closest public county-level workaround.

Main assumptions:

* Income midpoints: $10k, $27.5k, $42.5k, $62.5k, $112.5k

* Cost-burden midpoints: 10%, 25%, 40%

* Debt-service share: 80%

* Remaining term: 25 years

* Zero/negative income bins are preserved but excluded from balance inference.

Documented in:

metadata/proxy_methodology_assumptions.csv





Aggregate Home Equity

Variable:

acs_implied_aggregate_home_equity_proxy

Formula:

ACS owner-occupied housing value proxy - aggregate mortgage debt proxy

where:

owner-occupied housing value proxy = ACS owner-occupied units × ACS median home value

Why:

There is no public LA County household balance-sheet series for aggregate home equity. This follows the sheet’s workaround: housing value minus debt.





Monthly Mortgage Payment

Variables:

mortgage_payment_3_5pct_down mortgage_payment_10pct_down mortgage_payment_20pct_down

Formula:

monthly principal-and-interest payment = amortized loan payment on Redfin median sale price

Assumptions:

* Down payment scenarios: 3.5%, 10%, 20%

* Loan term: 30 years

* Rate: Freddie Mac PMMS monthly average 30-year fixed rate

* Excludes taxes, insurance, HOA, maintenance, and PMI

Why:

Actual realized mortgage payments are not public at LA County monthly frequency. These are affordability scenarios, not observed payments.





Downpayment

Variables:

down_payment_3_5pct down_payment_10pct down_payment_20pct

Formula:

Redfin median sale price × down payment share

Why:

Down payment amounts are not publicly observed by county/month. These scenarios represent common affordability benchmarks: FHA-style low down, intermediate, and conventional 20%.

Closing Costs

Variables:

closing_cost_2pct closing_cost_3pct closing_cost_5pct

Formula:

Redfin median sale price × closing cost share

Why:

Public geography-specific closing-cost data are not available. The percentages give low/central/high scenario estimates.





Single-Family Housing Starts proxy

Variable:

permits_1_unit

How used:

Census BPS 1-unit permits are used as the public proxy for single-family starts.

Why:

The available public source is permits, not starts or completions. The database explicitly labels this as permits and does not relabel permits as starts.





Net Migration / Immigration workaround

Main variable:

irs_soi_net_migration_exemptions

Formula:

IRS SOI in-migration exemptions - IRS SOI out-migration exemptions

Also loaded:

irs_soi_net_migration_returns irs_soi_net_migration_agi

Why:

IRS SOI county migration is the official public annual county migration workaround. Exemptions approximate people better than returns. Returns approximate filing units/households. AGI captures income composition.

Limitation:

This is tax-return migration, not complete immigration. It misses nonfilers and does not fully capture international migration.





Price-to-Rent Ratio

Variable:

price_to_rent_ratio

Formula:

ZHVI / (12 × ZORI)

Why:

This gives a monthly price-to-annual-rent affordability ratio. It uses the active Zillow ZORI smoothed seasonally adjusted series. This is implemented as an alternate source version; the FHFA/ZORI version from the sheet is still not separately constructed.
