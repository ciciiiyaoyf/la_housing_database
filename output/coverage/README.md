# Coverage and missingness reports

Generated reports describing what observations exist across sources, dates,
variables, and geographies. Source, time, ZIP, missingness, BPS archive, Redfin
vintage, and related reports explain partial coverage and source gaps.

Coverage is evidence for the local build, not a guarantee of current provider
releases. Read non-null variable coverage as well as overall table date ranges.
These reports support validation and review; they are not housing observations
and must not be used to fill missing data.
